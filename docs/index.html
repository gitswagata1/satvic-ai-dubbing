Satvic AI Dubbing

This dubs a 16-minute Hindi health video into Telugu and Malayalam in the creator's own cloned voice. It chains five open models locally on a MacBook, at ₹0 API cost.

Pipeline

1 · DemucsSplit the voice from the music bed
→
2 · WhisperHindi transcript, repaired segment by segment
→
3 · TranslationLLM with a glossary, numbers spelled out, back-translated for review
→
4 · IndicF5AI4Bharat voice clone, one clip per segment
→
5 · ffmpegTime-fit each clip to its slot, remix the music, −14 LUFS

The hardest bug: the voice model sometimes echoed its own reference sentence into a segment. The fix was re-cutting the reference as a clean, complete sentence, plus a scan-and-regenerate loop that checks every segment. The pipeline.py runner in the repo times every stage and writes timings.json.
